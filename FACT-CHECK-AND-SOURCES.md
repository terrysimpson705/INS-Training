## Source limitations resolved

The AGCO alcohol landing page and several linked pages returned unavailable/not-found responses in the research tool. AGCO indexed materials were cross-checked against the current Ontario regulation and current LCBO guidance. Older LCBO PDFs contain superseded shelf-price and delivery statements; those were not treated as current instructions.

The current regulation confirms that the old single-contiguous-display subsections have been revoked. The course therefore presents the assigned INS layout as an operating instruction and uses the current category-specific container-share requirements. The regulation also includes conditional stock-transfer/warehouse arrangements; the course does not authorize any such arrangement or suggest a location has activated delivery. Only approved location-specific services are discussed.

## Owner confirmations

The owner approved the added alcohol, tobacco, accessibility and WHMIS topics; confirmed the existing INS training/retraining fees, audit charges and operating rules; and instructed that the existing supplier minimums, cutoffs, contact details and model-specific procedures remain as store procedures. These private operational details were not represented as independently verified public law. Do not apply a model-specific button sequence to another device or a supplier example to an unconfirmed account.

The owner also specified the Mr. Dairy product restriction: milk, eggs, cheese, yogurt, butter and other approved dairy items only. Every other product must be purchased through its authorized INS supplier. This is an INS purchasing rule, not a claim about which products the supplier sells.

## Six-hour estimate

The module plans total 360 minutes: 168 reading/review minutes, 76 practice minutes and 116 quiz minutes. The reading text contains about 23,000 words, plus pictures, tables and 29 expandable decision exercises. The estimate assumes active reading and use of the exercises; it is not a timed completion gate or a guarantee that every learner will take exactly six hours.
