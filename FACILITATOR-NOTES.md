## Corrections and questions for Operations

### Audit programs and fees

Levels to Excellence lists $250 + HST, while the planogram audit program lists a $1,000 audit cost. General defaults, Mystery Shop sales-registration failures, complaints and retraining have other schedules. Confirm application and combination with Operations. Do not automatically accumulate them.

### Food temperatures and sample forms

The older 38°C/100°F warmer instruction is not a safe hot-holding target for potentially hazardous food. The lessons use hot holding at least 60°C and cold holding no more than 4°C. Use food-specific cooking/reheating instructions rather than the source log’s universal 74°C statement. Source product shelf lives and equipment settings need program confirmation.

### Temperature-check frequency

The earlier operating text says refrigeration at opening/closing and prepared food three times daily. The sample log says at least three checks daily. The course adopts the stricter three-check routine pending equipment-specific Operations confirmation.

### Lottery payouts and wagering limits

Current OLG public guidance says stores must pay up to $50, and may pay under $1,000 depending on ability. The source slide’s $300 optional payout language and fixed appointment tiers are preserved as historical text, not current instructions. Check terminal procedures and any stricter INS cash limit. Source sports-wager limits need confirmation with current OLG products.

### Ontario alcohol pricing, training and returns

The annual fee, 3–6-week onboarding period and 10%/20% price formulas are historical planning details. Verify current fees, pricing and approved training. Confirm the store’s return/opt-in arrangement and required nearby-return signage. Container size/type, not a universal beer/wine distinction, determines deposits.

### Stock depth, prices and display dimensions

Six per peg/six deep where space allows conflicts with a three-item minimum on another page. The .09 price-ending wording is ambiguous. General case-display dimensions differ from a beverage example. Use the approved category and store-specific plan.

### Uniforms, seating and suppliers

Black pants appear in the compliance checklist, while the service deck permits knee-length black shorts. Wearwell and Agile appear in different uniform references. Confirm current options. Apply required accommodation arrangements to seating, hydration and other workplace rules.

### Ordering windows and territory assignments

Tobacco ordering is described as permitted only on the delivery date. Confirm the assigned order window and cutoff. Slush Puppie east/west and downtown labels conflict. Confirm the assigned distributor/manager. Magnum lists separate order-desk and asset-tag service routes.

### Prepaid thresholds and dated hardware terms

The supplier calls $500/five cards both maximums and KYC thresholds. Pause for authorized direction; do not split sales or assume extra ID permits exceeding them. Confirm the 20-day hardware-return and $30 shipping terms because the source February 28 date has no year.

### Camera access and credentials

Default/shared POS or supervisor codes and camera device identifiers are excluded. Obtain store-specific access privately from the authorized trainer/administrator. The generic setup steps remain in the course. Original camera QR screenshots are not packaged.

### Linked work-computer examples

The work chat supplied October 2026 beverage case/unit-cost and order-status examples. They are historical calculation and receiving examples, not current price quotes, landed-cost verification or new company-wide approval policy. Its broad local file search returned no usable results.

### Pacing, assessment and certification

The 120-minute schedule is a guided design estimate, not a forced timer or validated average completion time. The course’s 80% threshold and retries support learning. Formal INS certification, practical observation and AGCO-approved training remain separate.
